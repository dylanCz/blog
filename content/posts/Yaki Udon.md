- Prep Time: 5 mins
- Cook Time: 10 mins
- Servings: 3
## Ingredients:
- Udon noodles 
- Beef/Pork strips
- Half an onion
- A few spring onions 
- 40ml cup dark soy sauce
- 30ml oyster sauce
- 25g brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon mirin
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- ½ teaspoon rice wine vinegar
## Directions:
1. Slice the onion and spring onions 
2. Combine all liquids and sugar for sauce 
3. Start boiling the udon, remove and strain after 3 mins
4. Start frying meat and onion for a few minutes, and then add the sauce 
5. Add noodles and mix 
### Notes
This was nice but the sauce was a bit too rich/strong. Maybe recommend reducing the amount or using light soy sauce instead of dark.
