Here's the obsidian plugins I currently use and what I use them for!
## Editing Toolbar by Cuman
A really nice toolbar that sits above the title of each note. Makes formatting incredibly intuitive. 
## Excalidraw by Zsolt Viczian
Excalidraw, in Obsidian! 
## Git by Vinzent
Git, so I can sync notes to my website, see [[Publishing Obsidian Notes to a Website]]. 
## Linter by Vincent Tao
A nice linter for auto-formatting and frontmatter creation. 
## Notebook Navigator by Johan Sanneblad
A nicer approach to folder structure.
