Prep Time: 5 mins
Cook Time: 15 mins
Servings: 2
## Ingredients:
- tin of chopped tomatoes
- 4 cloves of garlic
- 1 ball of mozzarella
- diced chorizo
- spaghetti
- parmesan
- 1tbsp oregano
- 1tbsp chili flakes
## Directions:
1. Fry the chorizo in olive oil until the fat is rendered and it’s crispy then set to the side
2. Cook the tomatoes and garlic in the chorizo oil
3. Blend the sauce with the mozzarella then stick it back in the pan
4. Add salt, pepper and chili flakes and loosen with some pasta water
5. Add cooked pasta and chorizo, mix and serve
### Notes
Banger dish, serves 2 portions.
