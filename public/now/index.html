What I’m doing now#

Hello! My name is Dylan Clark and I’m currently a Senior DevOps Engineer from Scotland. I work at Smarter Grid Solutions with an incredible team of people to help customers manage their DER (distributed energy resources). I’m part of the DevOps team, where I focus on automating and optimizing our infrastructure and deployments.

Before joining the DevOps team, I worked as a Software Engineer at Smarter Grid Solutions. I was involved in the development of Java Springboot micro-services, as we transitioned our flagship product from a monolithic architecture to a microservices architecture.

When I’m not focusing on my career, there’s a good chance I’m doing something related to competitive gaming. I’ve been playing Super Smash Brothers Melee competitively for over 10 years now and have travelled all over the world just to compete.

If you want to reach out to me, you can find me on BlueSky, Github and LinkedIn!
