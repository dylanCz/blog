Updates#

  • Well, Pokemon Champions is interesting… I was quite looking forward to see what Nintendo would cook for the official battle simulator, and while I think the general idea was well-founded, I can’t help but feel the execution and decision making is poor. It’s so much faster than S/V so tournaments will be significantly nicer to watch and the free to play rewards are incredibly generous. But there’s no teamsheet support and ranked is of course Bo1. Most egregiously there’s no way to save replays? Definitely a little bit disappointing, will see if Nintendo pull anything out of the bag over the next few months.
